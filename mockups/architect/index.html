architect flow · mockup set

the architect's two modes and their states

Nine screens, one visual language

Static pages — no API. The layout keeps three things visible at all times: what story or chapter is being prepared, what the proposed definition looks like, and what the architect most recently asked, changed, or failed to do. On mobile the proposal comes before the conversation.

scaffold — the `--new` interview
step 1 · modal The idea step

Rough idea, model choice, and how it proposes: stage by stage (staged) or the whole story at once (oneshot). Overlays the empty story editor.

 step 2 · staged, gate open Story gate proposal

The checklist renders above the round: story / cast / settings / scene. Title, premise, tension and facts have landed; approve & continue opens the cast gate.

 step 3 · question pins its gate A standing question

The FIRST DECIDE gate failed to land — the architect asks instead of proposing. Everything typed is the answer; the approve button hides until it is answered.

 step 4 · mid-checklist Cast gate landed

Story ticked, cast proposed with the three psychology fields, settings gate now open. Refinement never advances a gate; only approve does.

 step 5 · accept Folder-name step

`needs_folder` — accept does not disappear until the server answers `kind:"written"`. Nothing is on disk yet.
handoff — preparing the next chapter
state 1 · busy Preparing

`.thinking` shows; Accept and Send are disabled; Abandon stays available. A second round in flight would be a 409.

 state 2 · edits available Four separate lists

`applied` as labelled before → after lines, `ignored`, advisory `flags`, and `problems` — four different blocks, never merged.

 state 3 · pendingAsk An unanswered question

Visually distinct from an ordinary suggestion. It blocks a meaningful proposal until answered, but writes nothing.

 state 4 · written Accepted

`story.json` rewritten and preflighted. Offers to write the prepared chapter via /select — the handoff itself never starts a run.
